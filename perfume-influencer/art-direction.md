# House art direction: Romantic Heritage

Working names: **Romantic Heritage** or **Moody English Eclectic**. Romantic Dark Academia + English Manor + Moody Traditional / Heritage Maximalism.

The common thread is not "dark." It is **old-world, collected, saturated, intellectual and warm.** Rooms look like they evolved over generations, not like they were decorated all at once.

**The line we hold: moody, not gothic.** Darkness and drama, but the warmth, florals, velvet, books, glowing lamps and earthy reds make it cocooning rather than spooky. No cold stone, no crypts, no skulls-and-candelabra, no blue-grey light.

## Ingredients

- **Architectural depth:** wall paneling, substantial crown molding, arched openings, built-in bookcases, millwork painted the same color as the walls.
- **Deep, earthy jewel tones:** oxblood, burgundy, tobacco, mahogany, forest and olive green, midnight blue, black-brown, rust, mustard, muddy blush.
- **Dark warm woods:** walnut, mahogany, cherry, aged oak. Never pale natural wood.
- **Low amber lighting:** shaded lamps, picture lights, sconces, candles, chandeliers, brass fixtures. Almost no bright overhead light.
- **Traditional furniture with weight:** Chesterfield shapes, leather club chairs, velvet upholstery, antique writing desks, substantial wood tables.
- **Pattern and patina:** Persian rugs, florals, tapestries, vintage paintings, aged books, brass, worn leather, objects that look inherited.
- **Romantic elements:** enormous floral art, burgundy walls, velvet curtains, candlelight and warm pinks, so it never tips into a masculine "gentleman's club."
- **Intentional abundance:** books, art and objects everywhere, arranged rather than cluttered.

## Prompt block (append to every room or scene prompt)

> Romantic heritage interior in an old English manor: paneled walls and built-ins painted the same deep jewel tone, substantial crown molding, dark warm woods (walnut, mahogany, aged oak), Persian rugs, velvet and worn leather, aged books, brass fixtures, vintage oil paintings and florals, objects that look inherited, arranged not cluttered. Low amber light from shaded lamps, picture lights, sconces and candles, no overhead lighting. Warm, saturated, intellectual, cocooning, moody not gothic. Photorealistic 35mm film photograph.

**Negative cues to avoid:** cold stone, grey or blue light, gothic cathedral, skulls, cobwebs, minimalism, pale wood, white walls, bright overhead lighting, modern furniture.
