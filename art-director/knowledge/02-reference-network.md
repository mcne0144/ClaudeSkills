**Sourcing key.** T1 = primary (the person's or company's own site or post). T2 = trade or news press. T3 = secondary (LinkedIn, Wikipedia, aggregators, third-party summaries). Canon = widely documented, not re-fetched this session. Self-reported numbers are labeled **(claim)**. Where identity could not be confirmed, the entry says **unverified**. Research date: 2026-10-03.

## 1. Taste, design, and creative-culture commentators

### Tommy Geoco (@designertom), "This Is Taste"
- **Who:** Designer, filmmaker, YouTuber; co-host of Dive Radio; co-founder of Inflight.co (Threads bio, T3); founder of Enjoyers Media (LinkedIn, T3). Says he spent "15+ years in tech and design," is "former military," and a father of five (State of Play podcast description, T3). Associated with UX Tools (uxtools.co, T1).
- **Known for:** *This Is Taste*, a self-funded docuseries of 15 to 20 minute episodes following creative teams on location for 48 hours (Geoco LinkedIn 2025-08-31, T3). Season 1 episodes released by May 2026: Michael Riddering, Soren Iverson, Colin and Samir, with Vercel, Metalab, and Perplexity announced (Geoco LinkedIn 2026-05-23, T3). Episode 1 premiered Monday, October 27 (UX Tools episode page, T1; year implied 2025).
- **Key quote:** He expected taste to be "about the details... The little craft decisions," and wrote "I was wrong" after the Colin and Samir episode (Geoco Substack 2026-05-22, T1).
- **Lesson:** Taste shows up in how a team decides, not just in surface polish. When critiquing, ask about the decision process behind the frame.

### Eugene Healey
- **Who:** Melbourne-based brand strategy consultant, educator, and creator; formerly Associate Strategy Director at The Contenders and Head of Growth at Lively (Peter Spear interview, That Business of Meaning, 2025-01-06, T3). Lectured at the University of Melbourne postgraduate business school; clients named include Google, Spotify, Red Bull, FEELD, Chick-fil-A (eugenehealey.com, T1; LinkedIn, T3). Following "over 500,000" (eugenehealey.com, T1, **claim**).
- **Known for:** Culture analyzed "through the lens of brand"; the **brand-as-mosaic** metaphor (no single point of origin for brand experience in fragmented media); "semiotic dissonance" (e.g., Liquid Death taking rock codes into water) (Spear interview, T3). Runs a 7-week course, *The New Fundamentals of Brand* (eugenehealey.com, T1). 2026 series on the "post-social era" (LinkedIn 2026-09-14, T3).
- **Lesson:** Judge ideas for **scalability across fragmented moments**, not just for one hero execution. Brands are now worlds with multiple entry points.

### Mark Pollard, "Strategy Is Your Words"
- **Who:** Australian strategist, runs Mighty Jungle and Sweathead (strategy training). Started in agencies at 19; brand planning at Leo Burnett Sydney; ran strategy at Big Spaceship; published Australia's first full-color hip hop magazine, *Stealth* (Google Books author bio, T3).
- **Known for:** *Strategy Is Your Words: A Strategist's Fight for Meaning* (Mighty Jungle, 2020, 400 pages) (Google Books, T3). Kickstarter raised $37,000; 6,000 hardcovers sold (Amazon listing, **claim**); "5,000 copies" stated earlier on Sweathead (T1), so counts vary by date. **The Four Points**: Problem, Insight, Advantage, Strategy (Pollard on JustCreative, 2024-11-20, T2/T3).
- **Lesson:** "In strategy, words are all that matters" (markpollard.net, T1). Write the problem and insight as sentences before drawing anything. See file 10, Sections 2, 10, 11.

### Stuff About Advertising (Ashley Rutstein)
- **Who:** Ashley Rutstein, Denver-based freelance creative director and copywriter (since July 2021), previously ACD at a Denver agency (LinkedIn, T3). Headline lists "Adweek Creative 100" (LinkedIn, T3, **claim**).
- **Known for:** @stuffaboutadvertising on TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube: campaign breakdowns, industry commentary, concepting and portfolio tips; "more than 150k followers" (LinkedIn, **claim**). Teaches portfolio school and university students (stuffaboutadvertising.com, T1).
- **Lesson:** Explain why an ad works in plain language. The agent should be able to do a 60-second "here's why this works" breakdown of any campaign.

### Rob (Robert) Mayhew
- **Who:** UK creative with 20+ years at London agencies including Grey London and Ogilvy, then UK Head of Influence and Retail at FleishmanHillard (Performance Marketing World 2023-03-23, T2). Joined Gravity Road as Creative Director in March 2023 to shape TikTok content (Mayhew LinkedIn 2023-03-16, T3).
- **Known for:** Agency-life comedy sketches built on stand-up skills; about 140,000 TikTok followers at the time; The Drum called him "Adland's favourite social media star" (Marketing Week 2023, T2; Advertising Week EU 2023, T1).
- **Lesson:** Specific, insider truth is funny and shareable. Start in his forties; "You are not too old and you are not too late" (Creativebrief BITE 2023-06-15, T2).

### Zachary Winterton
- **Who:** Portland, Oregon creative director; worked at Laundry Service, AKQA, and Wieden+Kennedy; founded his studio Winterton in 2021 (Did I Do That? podcast guest page, T1/T3). LinkedIn lists a current CD role at OKX (T3).
- **Known for:** Work for Nike, Adobe, Netflix, LVMH, Arc'teryx, Microsoft (zacharywinterton.com, T1); Managing Editor of *The Creative Brief* newsletter (podcast page, T3).

### Brian Collins (COLLINS)
- **Who:** Co-founder and CCO of COLLINS, founded 2008 (Wikipedia, T3) with Leland Maschmeyer (D1S1, T3). Previously ran Ogilvy & Mather's Brand Integration Group (BIG) for about ten years (Wikipedia, T3; briancollins.design, T1).
- **Known for:** Mailchimp, Dropbox, Twitch, Robinhood identities (COLLINS case studies, T1); widely shared talks on design and optimism (Canon).
- **Lesson:** "Extrapolate a beloved characteristic in the product into a larger value" (COLLINS Dropbox case, T1). Find the product truth people already love and scale it into the brand.

### Jason Bagley (ASAP)
- **Who:** Former CCO of Wieden+Kennedy Portland; 16 years at W+K (MM+M 2025-05-27, T2).
- **Known for:** Founded the **Audacious School of Astonishing Pursuits (ASAP)** in 2021 (Ad Age 2021-10-04, T2). Courses include "Creative Megamachine" and "Art Director Megamachine" (with Danilo Boer and Lawrence Melilli); 700+ creatives completed courses; invitation-only year-round **ASAP Guild** launched 2025 (MM+M, T2). Brand designed as "a loving parody of the advertising world" (Webby entry, T1).

### Nathan Williams, *The Eye*
- **Who:** Founder and creative director of *Kinfolk*; Chief Creative Officer of Indigo from June 2019 (nathanwilliams.com, T1).
- **Known for:** *The Eye: How the World's Most Influential Creative Directors Develop Their Vision* (Artisan, Oct 30, 2018), 90+ creative directors including Thom Browne, Fabien Baron, Grace Coddington (Hachette, T1; Creative Review 2018-10-25, T2).
- **Lesson:** An "eye" is built from specific inputs (books, mentors, places). Keep a documented reference diet.

### Rick Rubin, *The Creative Act*
- **Who:** Record producer (Def Jam co-founder, American Recordings) (Canon).
- **Known for:** *The Creative Act: A Way of Being* (Penguin Press, January 2023) (Canon): creativity as attention, receptivity, and editing; the "source" of ideas is outside the self.
- **Lesson:** Taste is the ability to notice and to cut. Present fewer, better options.

### David Hockney, joiners
- **Who:** British painter (Canon).
- **Known for:** **Joiners**, photocollages begun in February 1982 as Polaroid grids, later freer compositions, because a single photograph felt "too devoid of life" (Morgan Library, T1). Major work: *Pearblossom Hwy., 11-18th April 1986* (Getty Museum, T1; hockney.com, T1).
- **Lesson:** Many viewpoints in one frame convey time and movement. A useful device for carousels, product storytelling, and place-based brands.

### Derrick Gee
- **Who:** Sydney-based music commentator, DJ, radio host; studied design at UTS; worked in TV at Fremantle; launched Finetooth Radio in 2012; residencies on NTS (2016-20), Spiritland, SiriusXM; led Mixcloud's brand partnerships division LOUD; creative director at 88rising (derrickgee.com, T1).

First TikTok in January 2021 got 300k views (his Substack 2023-08-01, T1); his website says his creator journey began "early 2022" (T1). Note the discrepancy. About 437K TikTok followers (TikTok profile, T3).
- **Lesson:** Generosity and context ("I don't gate keep") build trust. Explain the references, don't just drop them.

### Switched On Pop
- **Who:** Musicologist Nate Sloan (USC) and songwriter Charlie Harding (Vox Media Podcast Network, T1).
- **Known for:** A Vulture podcast about "the making and meaning of popular music" (Vulture, T2); book *Switched On Pop: How Popular Music Works and Why It Matters* (Oxford University Press, T1). Vulture reports a Netflix debut "July 14" (Vulture, T2; year not confirmed in source excerpt).
- **Lesson:** Break a hit into its parts (tension, hook, repetition) to understand why it works. Do the same with visuals.

### Daniel Wall (and "Undercurrent")
- **Who:** Daniel Wall (@danielswall), Scottsdale-based content creator; TikTok 2.2M+ followers (KOLens, T3; TikTok bio, T3). ### Marques Brownlee (MKBHD)
- **Who:** Tech YouTuber, channel started 2009 (Canon).
- **Known for:** Very high production polish in tech reviews (clean sets, matte red accent, precise macro shots). In September 2024 his Panels wallpaper app drew backlash over pricing and data disclosures (Canon, verify before citing).
- **Lesson:** A consistent visual signature (lighting, color, framing) becomes a brand asset. Even trusted creators lose goodwill fast when a product feels extractive.

### Casey Neistat

- **Known for:** Nike "Make It Count" (2012), a daily vlog run (2015-16), co-founding Beme (acquired by CNN in 2016) (Canon). Appears in Tommy Geoco's *This Is Taste* production story ("Casey Neistat in the room," UX Tools, T1).

### Kraig Adams

### Canoopsy

### Yasin (Untapped Creativity)

## 2. Studios, brands, and makers

### Front Office (@frontoffice.co)
- **Who:** Melbourne fashion brand with a studio at 63 Smith Street, Fitzroy (frontoffice.co, T1). Founder name **unverified** in sources checked.
- **Known for:** "A clothing brand created by study": designs drawn from workwear, military uniforms, and sportswear, where "every item has a historical reference that we honour and/or subvert" (frontoffice.co, T1). Produces video on garment design history across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Substack; also offers agency advisory work to fashion clients (frontoffice.co positions and agency pages, T1).
- **Lesson:** Research is content. Showing the reference behind the product makes the product feel inevitable.

### Nikolas (Nik) Bentel, Oatly collab
- **Who:** New York designer; Brown (modern culture) and RISD (industrial design) dual degree; brother Lukas Bentel is a co-creative director of MSCHF (Hypebeast 2024-07-26, T2).
- **Known for:** "Performative objects" such as the viral Pasta Bag; Nik Bentel Studio designs, manufactures, and distributes; about half its revenue comes from brand collaborations (Hypebeast, T2). Collaborations include MSCHF, Absolut, COS, LeBron James, Hennessy, Lidl (studio site, T1) and **The Oatly Matcha Urgency Bag** (studio collaborations page, T1).
- **Lesson:** A physical object with a joke built in can be a whole campaign. Brands should "treat creators as creatives NOT influencers" (post on Nik Bentel Studio LinkedIn citing Dan Salkey, 2025-11, T3).

### Tom Sachs
- **Who:** New York sculptor known for bricolage and his studio as an art practice (Curbed 2023-03-13, T2).
- **Known for:** *Ten Bullets* (2010), an employee-handbook film directed by Van Neistat, with rules like parallel and 90-degree placement (Curbed, T2); long Nike collaboration including Mars Yard shoes (Artnet 2023-03-21, T2).
- **Caveat:** In 2023 former employees alleged an abusive workplace (Curbed, T2; Artnet, T2). Nike did not move forward with his sneaker deal and Sachs apologized: "I deeply regret that anyone, ever, felt less than supported, safe and fulfilled within my studio" (NYT 2023-05-10, T2).
- **Lesson:** Systems and rituals can become the brand (Sachs called the studio culture "his greatest work of art," per Curbed). Cite the craft, never the management model.

### Cosmos (cosmos.so) and Public Work
- **Who:** Founded by Andy McCune (previously Unfold, acquired by Squarespace) with Luca Marra, started in 2021 as a side project (WWD 2026-03-17, T2; McCune LinkedIn, T3).
- **Known for:** A Pinterest alternative for creatives without likes or comments, AI tagging, "clusters" (Airnauts case study, T3). $6M seed from GV and Accel (announced June 2023, McCune LinkedIn, T3); $15M Series A co-led by Shine Capital and Matrix (January 2026, Cosmos blog, T1). Named one of Apple's 25 apps of 2025 (WWD, T2). McCune describes it as "a taste graph you can search, explore, and shop" (LinkedIn 2026-09-10, T3, **claim**). **public.work** is "Public Work by Cosmos" (public.work, T1).
- **Lesson:** Curation is authorship. Credit and context for images matter (McCune's stated motivation, WWD, T2).

### Uncommon Creative Studio (The Ordinary "The Periodic Fable")
- **Who:** London creative company; co-founder Nils Leonard quoted on the work (The Drum 2025-10-15, T2).
- **Known for:** *The Periodic Fable* for The Ordinary: 49 beauty buzzwords as a periodic table, a classroom film by Olivia De Camps, OOH, and a permanent web tool; D&AD Black Pencil (D&AD, T1; One Club, T1). Full breakdown in file 10, Section 9.
- **Lesson:** Restraint and a borrowed format can expose a whole category.

### Isle of Any (Coinbase)
- **Who:** New York creative company run by founders and CCOs Laurie Howell and Toby Treyer-Evans (shots 2026-03-12, T2; It's Nice That 2025-05-07, T2).
- **Known for:** Coinbase "System Update" blue-screen ASCII spots for the 2025 NBA Playoffs (with Buck) (It's Nice That, T2); the 2026 Super Bowl singalong "Everybody Coinbase" (shots, T2); and "Your Way Out" for the 98th Academy Awards, March 2026, directed by Oscar Hudson, with PS2-era game worlds built in camera (Adweek 2026-03-12, T2); D&AD Black Pencil (D&AD, T1).
- **Lesson:** "We liked the idea of making the experience of seeing the work as part of the message" (Howell, It's Nice That, T2). Design the media moment, not just the ad.

### Gilga (Donald Glover)
- **Who:** Donald Glover's creative studio, founded 2023, based on a farm in Ojai, California (BlkScript, T3; GQ via Vuink 2023-04-08, T3).
- **Known for:** Described by Glover as "Erewhon for culture" and "the filter" for low-quality phone culture; named after Gilgamesh; job postings in 2023 included "Acquisitions + Taste" (The FADER 2023-04-10, T2). Filmmaking collaboration shown in Google's Veo video (YouTube, 2024-05-14, T1).
- **Lesson:** Taste as a business model: curating and filtering is a service.

### Gap campaign team (Invisible Dynamics, Bethany Vargas, Zoi Tatopoulos)
- **Invisible Dynamics:** brand transformation consultancy founded by CEO Oli Walsh; created and produced Gap's Fall 2025 **"Better in Denim"** with KATSEYE set to Kelis's "Milkshake"; "Creative Concept, Content Strategy, and Art Direction by Invisible Dynamics and Gap" (Invisible Dynamics, T1; Forbes 2025-09-05, T2). Forbes describes a two-year Gap brand transformation with Walsh's team (T2).
- **Bethany Vargas:** director of "Better in Denim" (Gap Inc. 2025-08-19, T1); previously photographer and creative director for Marina, Kali Uchis, the Marías; directed Lady Gaga's "Abracadabra" video (The Cut 2025-10-03, T2). ### Skylrk (Finn Rush-Taylor) and Serifa
- **Skylrk:** Justin Bieber's fashion label, debut collection July 2025, with business partner Neima Khaila (Geo.tv 2025-07-11, T3; Milk HK 2025-07-18, T3).
- **Finn Rush-Taylor:** British designer, Skylrk design lead since October 2022; previously Puma, adidas, Crocs; specializes in 3D and 3D-printed footwear (The Media Eye 2025-07-17, T3; LinkedIn, T3). ### Szilveszter Makó
- **Who:** Hungarian fashion photographer and director, born 1992 in Miskolc, MOME graduate, based in Milan (Wikipedia, T3).

### Heidi Caillier
- **Who:** AD100 interior designer, Seattle-based, no formal training (heidicaillierdesign.com, T1).

### Geoff Lowe (Olympics art)

### Rich Tu

- **Known for:** ADC Young Guns; Paul Manship Medallion; co-founder of the COLORFUL awards with The One Club; professor at SVA (richtu.com, T1). Speaks on creativity and AI workflows (LinkedIn, T3).

### Jon Bellion (fan community)

## 3. Strategists and marketers

### Scott Milano (Tanj)
- **Who:** Founder and Managing Director of Tanj, an NYC naming and verbal identity studio; 15+ years in verbal identity (tanj.co, T1).
- **Known for:** Names like Actual and Flute (LinkedIn 2026, T3). Warns AI naming produces sameness and crowded trademark searches (LinkedIn 2026-07-17, T3).
- **Lesson:** Present names in context with a story of why. A familiar word with a new job can beat an invented one. See file 10, Section 4.

### Dan Salkey (Small W•rld)
- **Who:** Co-founder and Strategy Partner of Small W•rld, London; ex-adidas (LinkedIn, T3; Zencastr On Brand podcast 2025-04-21, T3). Mother took a minority stake in 2024 (LBB, T2; Salkey LinkedIn 2024-02-12, T3).
- **Known for:** "Entertain or Die" philosophy and book; annual research with Tracksuit, 2026 edition surveyed 16,200 US consumers (LBB, T2). Pays creators day rates "to work as actual creatives," up to about 20% of a project team from its creator pool (LBB, T2; LinkedIn 2026-01-28, T3). Reported 283% growth over three years (LBB, **claim**).
- **Lesson:** "Treat creators as creatives NOT influencers." Hire creators to write, direct, and edit, not only to post.

### Zoe Scaman (Bodacious)
- **Who:** Founder of Bodacious (founded 2019), a strategy studio; previously at Droga5, Naked Communications, Undercurrent, and Global Head of Strategy at Ridley Scott Creative Group; CSO of Luka Dončić's 77X and his foundation (Wavelength, T3; BIMA 2025-05-23, T1). Inducted into BIMA Hall of Fame, May 2025 (BIMA, T1).
- **Known for:** Strategy at the edge of entertainment, fandom, and creator economies; Substack writing (substack.com/@zoescaman, T1). Healey cites her as a strategist who took the practice "into completely different disciplines" (Post-Culture 2026-03-20, T3).
- **Lesson:** Fandom is a business model. Design brand systems people can participate in.

### Jordan Rogers (sports marketing)
- **Who:** Former Nike brand marketer (Brand Marketing Director, Nike Basketball; Sr. Brand Manager, Nike Football, including Odell Beckham Jr.'s brand; Nike Women North America), who built his career after recovering from heroin addiction and incarceration (LinkedIn, T3; jordanrogers.xyz, T1). Founded Perseus Creative in 2022 (LinkedIn, T3).
- **Known for:** Sports marketing commentary, personal branding workshops for athletes; says he advises Meta (Instagram) on NIL and sports strategy (jordanrogers.xyz, T1, **claim**).
- **Lesson:** Sport plus culture: athletes are brands, and story is the strongest asset.

### Alex Garcia (Olipop analysis)
- **Who:** Content strategist who builds "social shows" and content teams for 8 to 9 figure brands (LinkedIn, T3). Not an Olipop employee per sources checked.
- **Known for:** "Olipop's $200M Content Playbook" posts (LinkedIn 2024-01-22 and 2024-01-24, T3). Related third-party write-up: Olipop hired creator Sara Crane to run its TikTok as a creator (marketingexamined 2024-01-09, T3). Olipop's former Head of Creative Keiji Ando reports sales grew "from $200M to $500M+" during his tenure (keijiando.com, T1, **claim**).
- **Lesson:** Brand accounts that behave like a creator (a face, a format, a series) outperform brand accounts that behave like ads. Build recurring "shows," not one-offs.

**Usage notes**
- **Reference hunting:** start with ShotDeck or shot.cafe for film language, FILMGRAB for free browsing, frameset for search-by-image, Same Energy and Cosmos for adjacent aesthetics, Are.na for building an argued collection.
- **Technique naming:** EYECANDY to find the name of a camera move or edit before writing it into a brief.
- **Lo-fi ideation and comps:** PhotoFunia and CoolText for deliberately cheap, fast, ironic comps (useful for meme-native or Y2K aesthetics); tooooools for dithering, halftone, and texture effects.
- **Presentation:** Maneken for in-situ mockups; Firefly Boards for collaborative mood boards with generated fills. Label AI-generated references clearly in client decks.
- **Ideation:** Deck of Brilliance for structured prompts when stuck.
- Licensing: same.energy notes its default search covers CC-BY images that require attribution (same.energy, T1). Film stills from ShotDeck, shot.cafe, FILMGRAB are for internal reference and decks, not for publication.
