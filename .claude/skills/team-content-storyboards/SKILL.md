# Team Content Storyboards

Produces one storyboard file per location per month, in the format proven in the Greenway month 2 system (`Gingerwolfe-Agency-Audit-Greenway/content/month-2/`). Read that folder first when it is available; it is the reference implementation.

## Inputs to gather before writing

1. **The location's last month of posts** with engagements and impressions. For Greenway: `Bright-Matter-HQ/tools/monday/data/<latest>/month/posts_summary.json` or the latest weekly `*-data.md`. Never write a "why" section without real numbers; if none exist, say so.
2. **Production stance** from the store's `STRATEGY.md` (full, full-ish, minimum viable, asset-led) and who films.
3. **Named on-camera people and consent status** from GM interview notes. Unknown names stay `[name]`; never invent a staff name.
4. **Open hold items:** pricing or fee policy, OEM wording, account access risks (`stores/MASTER_HANDLES.md`).
5. **Channels** that are live and connected.

## The three video types (every location, every week)

| Type | Job | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Connection | Make the location feel human and low pressure | Ask Anything interview, honest recommendation, team trend, meet the team, environment tour, day-in-the-life |
| School | Make the location useful, saved and searched | One customer question, under 45 s, fixed series title and cold open ("[Question]? Here's the honest answer.") |
| Story | Sell without selling | Product with a person, framed as the customer's life, plus one honest trade-off |

## Storyboard block format (repeat per video)

```
## SB-0N · "[Title]"
**Type:** · **Pillar:** · **Tier:** · **Length:**
**Why:** the stereotype or objection it fights + the location's own evidence
**On camera:** role + camera-shy level · **Shoot time:** · **Gear/vehicle:**
| Shot | Time | Frame | Action | On screen / spoken | Audio |
**Caption draft:**
**Checks:** consent, pricing/OEM holds, no competitor names, licensed audio
**Success read:** the one measure and its baseline
```

Each location file also carries: why this location this month (with numbers and sources), a weekly rhythm table, the camera-shy ladder or one-shoot week for low-capacity locations, decisions needed before Week 1, and a month-read table with baselines and "TO READ" cells.

## Rules

1. Label the file CLIENT-FACING DRAFT pending Shannon's approval. Nothing posts without her approval.
2. Two locations under one GM never share formats, hosts or lines.
3. No price, fee, rate, OEM, warranty or time claim on camera unless the location confirmed it in writing; mark such shots as holds.
4. Every figure carries its source and read date. Blank beats plausible.
5. No em dashes anywhere. Plain, numbered, phone-readable.
6. Low-capacity locations get one 20-minute shoot a week that yields four posts, and the camera-shy ladder (0 hands/voice, 1 background, 2 one line, 3 host).
7. If a format raises a ratified pillar share, list it as an operator decision.
8. After writing, scan for invented names, unsourced numbers and em dashes before handing over.
